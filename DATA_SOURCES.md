## Climate covariates

**Historical inputs.** The January 1880–December 2024 values of `NINO34`, `volc2` and `ssn` match the 1,740 monthly observations in Grant Foster’s *R code and data for method of Foster and Rahmstorf (2025)* (Zenodo, 6 June 2025). The archive identifies Grant Foster as creator and CC BY 4.0 as the licence. We used the observations from its `AdjustData.zip` archive and extended the series as described below. The upstream providers retain their applicable terms. [Versioned archive](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15608929) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · [Foster and Rahmstorf (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025GL118804)

- **ENSO.** HadISST data were obtained from the Met Office Hadley Centre and are © British Crown Copyright, Met Office 2003, provided under an Open Government Licence. We used the HadISST1 monthly SST fields to calculate the January 2025–August 2026 Niño 3.4 extension. We averaged the cells over 5°S–5°N and 170°W–120°W with cosine-latitude weights and aligned each calendar month to the historical series using the 1981–2010 overlap. The 20 extension values match the archived grid reconstruction. [Met Office Hadley Centre observations](https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/hadobs/) · [HadISST data](https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/hadobs/hadisst/) · [Provider terms](https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/hadobs/hadisst/terms_and_conditions.html) · [Open Government Licence v2](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/2/) · [Rayner et al. (2003)](https://doi.org/10.1029/2002JD002670)
- **Volcanic aerosols.** NASA Goddard Institute for Space Studies, Sato and Lacis stratospheric aerosol optical depth, global mean at 550 nm. The source ends in September 2012. Zeros from October 2012 through December 2024 are inherited from the Foster archive. We continued that convention through August 2026. [GISS source file](https://data.giss.nasa.gov/modelforce/strataer/original_GISS_data/tau.line_2012.12.txt) · [Sato et al. (1993)](https://doi.org/10.1029/93JD02553)
- **Solar activity.** Sunspot data from the World Data Center SILSO, Royal Observatory of Belgium, Brussels. We used monthly mean total sunspot number v2.0 (Clette and Lefèvre, 2015), licensed under CC BY-NC 4.0. January 2025–February 2026 uses a separate archived SILSO vintage; March–August 2026 uses the download of 16 September 2026. Values before March 2026 can differ from the September vintage. [SILSO Sunspot Number V2.0](https://doi.org/10.24414/qnza-ac80) · [Source CSV](https://www.sidc.be/SILSO/DATA/SN_m_tot_V2.0.csv) · [Provider credit and terms](https://www.sidc.be/SILSO/citations) · [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/)

## Packaged inputs

The monthly panel spans January 1880–August 2026. HadCRUT5 and the separately fitted arithmetic mean end in July 2026. The mean uses four records before 1940 and all five from January 1940, after rebasing each to 0.88 °C over 1991–2020. All applicable records must be observed in a given month. The annual exercise ends in 2025. The snapshot also supplies fitting parameters and derived quantities used by the published analyses.

The HadCRUT5 source file downloaded on 16 September 2026 has SHA-256 checksum `1cb8d36b1cf0a210a45476477e61c855555e0413f72f75dbc70b5d0c04ebacca`; its versioned URL is listed above. Checksums for the distributed files are in `data/SNAPSHOT.csv`.

Six unused columns in the analysis panels (`Berk_land`, `rssTLT`, `volc`, `mei`, `SOI`, `aero_erf`) contain only missing values wherever their names occur in this release, including in the embedded MATLAB tables. Their column positions support the table layouts expected by the estimation routines. These columns are not used in estimation.
